# Pricing Recommendation

## 1. Executive Summary

This document outlines a growth-focused pricing strategy for Apsics Media, designed to prioritize customer acquisition and cash flow generation while building a sustainable side business. The strategy leverages AI-enhanced speed advantages and trend intelligence to deliver weekly creative concepts at competitive price points. This approach targets digital subscription businesses seeking rapid creative testing capabilities with predictable weekly delivery.

## 2. Strategic Analysis

### 2.1 Key Business Constraints & Advantages

**Time Constraints:**
- 15-20 hours/week availability (side business model)
- Need for automated/AI-enhanced delivery processes
- Requirement for scalable service delivery

**Competitive Advantages:**
- AI-enhanced script generation (15 scripts in 15 minutes)
- Speed to market (weekly delivery vs industry standard 1-2 weeks)
- Trend intelligence and competitive analysis capabilities
- Performance marketing expertise from Fortune 500 background

**Market Positioning:**
- Target: Digital subscription services needing rapid creative testing
- Focus: Speed, volume, and trend intelligence over traditional agency consulting
- Differentiator: Weekly delivery cadence with trend-based concepts

## 3. Recommended Pricing Strategy

### 3.1. Service Model: Weekly Subscription Delivery

Transition from project-based to predictable weekly subscription model for consistent revenue and client retention.

### 3.2. Pricing Tiers

**Tier 1: Trend Tracker** - $67/month
- **Target Audience:** Small businesses and startups testing creative concepts
- **Weekly Delivery:**
  - 1 creative concept
  - 2 ready-to-develop scripts
  - Trend source attribution (TikTok, Instagram, etc.)
- **Time Investment:** ~5 minutes per client
- **Value Proposition:** Access to trending concepts at entry-level pricing

**Tier 2: Competitive Edge** - $197/month
- **Target Audience:** Growing D2C brands seeking competitive advantage
- **Weekly Delivery:**
  - 2 creative concepts (1 trend-based + 1 competitor-inspired)
  - 3 ready-to-develop scripts
  - Brief competitive context analysis
- **Time Investment:** ~8 minutes per client
- **Value Proposition:** Strategic intelligence combining trends + competitor analysis

**Tier 3: Market Intelligence** - $497/month
- **Target Audience:** Established businesses requiring comprehensive creative strategy
- **Weekly Delivery:**
  - 3 creative concepts (trend + competitor + original)
  - 4 ready-to-develop scripts
  - Weekly "what's working" insight summary
  - Direct team access for strategic support
- **Time Investment:** ~12 minutes per client + strategic consultation
- **Value Proposition:** Premium market intelligence with direct strategic access
