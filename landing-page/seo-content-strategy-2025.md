## SEO Technical Strategy

### On-Page Optimization:
- **Title Tags**: Include primary keyword + brand positioning
- **Meta Descriptions**: Focus on commercial intent and value proposition
- **H1/H2 Structure**: Logical hierarchy with keyword variations
- **Internal Linking**: Connect to service tiers and consultation forms
- **Schema Markup**: Article, FAQ, and HowTo structured data

### Content Strategy:
- **Word Count**: 2,500-4,000 words for pillar content
- **Keyword Density**: 1-2% primary, 0.5-1% secondary keywords
- **LSI Keywords**: Include semantic variations and related terms
- **Featured Snippets**: Target question-based queries with direct answers
- **Call-to-Actions**: Strategic consultation offers and lead magnets

### Technical Requirements:
- **Core Web Vitals**: LCP <2.5s, FID <100ms, CLS <0.1
- **Mobile Optimization**: Mobile-first indexing compliance
- **Page Speed**: Optimized images and minimal JavaScript
- **SSL Certificate**: HTTPS implementation across all pages
