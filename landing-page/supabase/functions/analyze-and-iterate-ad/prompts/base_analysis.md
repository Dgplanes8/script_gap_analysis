Workflow:
1. Confirm the creative format (video, static, carousel, or social URL) and note the length, pacing, and core message.
2. Identify strengths (hooks, proof, offers, storytelling) that should be preserved.
3. Diagnose weaknesses that create friction for attention, comprehension, or conversion.
4. Produce platform-native recommendations that map scenes, overlays, CTAs, and supporting assets to the requested output formats.
5. Package results with a concise executive summary, prioritized risks, and clearly actionable iteration packages.

Always preserve critical brand voice details and ensure CTAs align with the stated business goal. When duration or transcripts are unavailable, infer based on ad best practices and call out assumptions in recommendations.
