## Image Specifications

### Hero Section:
- Dimensions: 2000x1200px minimum
- Format: WebP for optimization
- Style: Professional, authoritative, clean

### Section Headers:
- Dimensions: 1200x600px minimum
- Format: WebP
- Style: Clean, modern, business-focused

### Icons/Badges:
- Dimensions: 400x400px minimum
- Format: SVG preferred, PNG acceptable
- Style: Clean, minimal, brand-consistent

## Usage Notes

1. All images should convey authority and professionalism
2. Color scheme should complement existing blue branding
3. Images must support "household brand" positioning
4. Avoid overly generic stock photo aesthetics
5. Ensure diverse representation in people-focused imagery
6. Maintain consistent professional tone across all visuals
