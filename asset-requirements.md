## Asset Specifications Summary

### Image Formats
- **Logos**: SVG preferred, PNG with transparency as fallback
- **Photos**: JPG for photographs, PNG for screenshots
- **Icons**: SVG for scalability

### Quality Standards
- **Resolution**: Minimum 2x for retina displays
- **Compression**: Optimized for web delivery (under 500KB per image)
- **Style**: Consistent professional look aligned with Framer design system

### Legal Requirements
- **Permissions**: Written consent for all client photos and company logos
- **Usage Rights**: Unlimited marketing use for Apsics Media
- **Attribution**: Credit requirements if any for stock photography
