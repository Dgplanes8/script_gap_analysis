## Personalization Variables

- **[Name]** - Use their actual first name
- **[Service Tier]** - Match to their company size/stage (Creative Starter, Trend Tracker, Competitive Edge, Market Intelligence)
- **[Specific detail]** - Recent funding, product launch, team hire, milestone
- **[Their industry]** - Specific vertical terminology (SaaS, E-commerce, FinTech, etc.)
- **[Specific result]** - Industry-relevant success metric or outcome
- **[Competitor reference]** - Name-drop relevant success stories in their space

---

## Performance Scoring Framework (25-Point Scale)

Each script element should be evaluated on:
1. **Attention Capture** (1-5): Will it stop the scroll?
2. **Emotional Resonance** (1-5): Triggers specific emotions?
3. **Benefit Clarity** (1-5): Core promise immediately clear?
4. **Call-to-Action Strength** (1-5): Creates urgency?
5. **Memorability** (1-5): Contains "sticky" elements?

**Decision Guide:**
- 21-25: Green light - exceptional potential
- 16-20: Proceed with optimizations
- Below 16: Rework - unlikely to perform
